Final widget — text translation only, compact control

Listen/TTS is removed: dynamic content can't be pre-generated, and per-request synthesis was the biggest cost leak. The panel goes with it — a native <select> replaces 324px of custom UI.

👉 Click "EN ▾" on the right — that's a real <select>. On a phone it opens the OS language picker.
