Footer right side — three dots vs lime dash

Everything else is settled (compact 44/18 lockup, clickable, tagline sweep, top divider). This is only about the right-hand element.

👉 The dots pulse on a loop; the dashes draw in on load. Hover the tagline too.
● A — THREE COLOUR DOTS (pulsing)
Blue / amber / green with a staggered pulse. My concern: these are the home page's per-subject hues — each colour there maps to a domain. In a footer they have no subject, so they look systematic while meaning nothing. Three dots also echo the WorkflowTerminal's window lights, so they can read as status indicators ("all systems green"), which the footer can't back.
