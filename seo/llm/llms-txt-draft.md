# WECARE.DIGITAL

> A network of microservice brands serving everyday Bharat across travel, paperwork, disputes, rituals, reflection, and support. Based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

WECARE.DIGITAL operates six microservice brands designed to solve real problems for real people with simple access, transparent pricing, and reliable service. Contact: one@wecare.digital | +919330994400 | Kolkata, West Bengal 700012, India.
