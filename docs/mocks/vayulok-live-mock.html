Health advisory

Moderate air — plan around the evening peak.

At AQI 168 the air sits in the Moderate band. Healthy adults will notice little, but sensitive groups should limit prolonged outdoor exertion today.

  • Keep windows shut from early evening, when the PM2.5 peak builds.
  • Prefer an indoor workout after 7 pm; the air is at its worst between 8 and 11 pm.
  • Children, older adults and anyone with asthma should carry their inhaler.
